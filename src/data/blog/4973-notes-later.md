I always wanted to write, but never made the time to launch a site and actually publish.

So most of my thoughts stayed buried in private notes. I have 4,973 Apple Notes going back to around 2018, with many more already lost to the void.

AI changed that.

Building the site, organizing ideas, and turning rough notes into something readable now has far less friction than it used to.

This is my attempt at sharing and preserving some of what I learn before it disappears too. Or before AI takes over and none of this matters.

Most of it will probably be field notes on AI tools, automation, developer tooling, homelab infrastructure, small hardware projects, and whatever else I end up learning the hard way.

Godspeed.
